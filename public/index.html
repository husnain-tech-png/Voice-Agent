📖 How to Connect Your Real Phone Number (In Simple Words)
1
Make your local server public: Open a new PowerShell terminal and run: ngrok http 3000 or npx localtunnel --port 3000
2
Save your public URL: Copy the https://... URL from ngrok and paste it into your .env file under PUBLIC_URL=https://your-domain.ngrok-free.app.
3
Attach to Twilio Number: Open your Twilio Console ➡️ Phone Numbers ➡️ Manage ➡️ Active Numbers ➡️ Click your phone number ➡️ Under "A CALL COMES IN", select Webhook (HTTP POST) and paste:
https://your-domain.ngrok-free.app/twilio/incoming
4
Pick up your cell phone and dial! The phone will ring, your AI assistant will answer with Bella's voice, and you can converse naturally with sub-second delay!
